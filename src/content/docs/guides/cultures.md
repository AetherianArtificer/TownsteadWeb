title: Cultures
description: How the culture of a town shapes its people's names, clothes and speech, and how cultures change over the years.
---

A culture is the way of life of a town. It shapes how its people are named, what they wear, what their town and its government are called, and how they talk to each other.

Cultures come from culture packs. Without a culture pack, villagers have no culture and are named by their region, as in MCA. The game shows them as "Village Common".

## What You Notice

- **Names.** People of a culture get names from its tradition. Some cultures pass down family names, and some build them from a parent's name, such as "Sigurdsson".
- **Town and faction names.** A town founded by a culture gets a name in its style, and so does its government.
- **Clothes.** Each culture prefers some clothes and materials. A town that has not proclaimed a livery uses the livery of its culture.
- **Speech.** With a culture voice, villagers use their culture's own greetings and words in conversations. See [Conversations & Dialogue](/guides/conversations/).

## Where Culture Comes From

A culture belongs to a community, not to a species. An elf raised in a fishing town of the fisherfolk is one of the fisherfolk.

- A child takes the culture of their parents.
- A newcomer takes the culture of the town they settle in.
- A town takes the culture of the first people who settle it, or of the founding profile it starts with.

## Cultures Change Over Time

People slowly take on the culture of the people around them: their town, their partner and close friends, and, for children, their parents. An adult who moves to a town of another culture takes on most of it in about three years. Children change faster.

A person can carry more than one culture. A villager raised by parents of two different cultures can speak and dress a little like both.

A town also shapes its people through what it builds. Some cultures have peoples within them, such as merchant houses and knightly courts. A town full of markets and docks draws its people toward the merchant ways. Its people keep their culture.

## Settings

In the Switchboard, **Systems > Cultures** turns cultures on or off for the world. With the **Peoples** dial at **Roots and Cultures**, the **Cultures** tab chooses which cultures new founders can be raised in, and how often.
