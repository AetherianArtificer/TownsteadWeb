---
title: Beverage Artisan
description: Baristas, bartenders, and brewers, and the mods that bring the drink trade.
---

The Beverage Artisan is Townstead's drink career. It has three paths, **Barista**, **Bartender**, and **Brewer**, and each exists only when a mod supplies its workplace. With no drink mod installed, the career has no workplaces and nobody takes it up.

## Where They Work

Each supported mod adds a workplace and the work done there. The Catalog lists the buildings and their requirements; in outline:

| Path | Workplaces | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barista | Cafés, from a corner café up to a grand coffee house | Rustic Delight |
| Barista | Tea stalls, tea rooms, and tea houses | Herbal Brews |
| Barista | Water purifier station | Tough As Nails |
| Bartender | Brew halls | Brewery |
| Bartender | Taproom | Brewin' and Chewin' |
| Bartender | Tavern | Kaleidoscope Tavern |
| Bartender | Restaurant | Candlelight |
| Bartender | Cocktail bar | Beachparty, Cocktails Delight |
| Brewer | Brew halls, taproom, and tavern | Brewery, Brewin' and Chewin', Kaleidoscope Tavern |

A villager is hired by the building, the same way a kitchen hires a cook. See [Cooking & Baking](/guides/cooking/#getting-a-cook). Which path they follow comes from the building: a café steers its worker toward Barista, a brew hall toward Bartender or Brewer.

## What They Make

Baristas brew coffee and tea at kettles, pots, and coffee stations, and roast beans where the mod has them. Bartenders mix drinks and cocktails. Brewers work kegs and brewing stations through the long processes: loading, watching, and drawing off the finished batch.

With a thirst mod installed, a Beverage Artisan also purifies water, at a campfire or skillet for Legendary Survival Overhaul's canteens and bottles, or at Tough As Nails' purifier. See [Thirst](/guides/needs/thirst/).

Coffee restores energy when a villager drinks it. See [Energy](/guides/needs/energy/#coffee-and-energy-items).

## Serving

Bartenders and baristas are the staff at [hangouts](/guides/hangouts/). At a tavern, the bartender serves the rounds. At a tea house, the barista serves the tea. A bartender or a cook serves supper. The worker must be assigned to that building.

## Orders

An [Order Sheet](/guides/orders/) in the workplace lists everything its stations can make. Use it to keep a drink in stock or to fill a batch order before the evening rush.

## Progression

The career has the same five ranks as Cook, and each path opens its own skills and title. Skills and ranks are on the career record at the Archives. See [Careers](/guides/professions/).

The Chronicle records a village's first brew.

## When They Stop

The worker says which of these is the problem when you are nearby: no ingredients, a particular ingredient missing, nowhere to put the output, no workplace, or a station they cannot reach.
