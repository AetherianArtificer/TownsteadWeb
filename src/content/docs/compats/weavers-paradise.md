**Author:** Vortianski\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/weavers-paradise) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/weavers-paradise)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **The Tailor career.** Weaver's Paradise makes the Tailor career available, and adds tailor's shops: Tailor's Corner, Tailor's Shop, and Clothier. See [Tailor](/guides/tailor/).
- **The spinning jenny.** Tailors spin fibre into spools.
- **The clothcrafting station.** Tailors weave cloth. A player plays a minigame to set the quality of the cloth. A Tailor's career rank sets it instead, so a master Tailor makes the best cloth.
- **Fabric.** Its cloth and spools count as fabric for a tailor's shop.

## With Other Mods

- **[Whole Cloth](/compats/whole-cloth/).** Four cloth scraps and an empty spool make a cotton spool at the spinning jenny.
