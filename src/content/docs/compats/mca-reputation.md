**Author:** Otectus\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mca-reputation)\
**Install on:** The server

## What It Adds

### Standing

The standing of a player in a village includes their MCA: Reputation score in that village. Each 10 points of reputation give 1 point of standing. The hearts of the residents and the player's deeds also give standing.

### Townstead Deeds

Townstead records these deeds in MCA: Reputation:

| Deed | When |
| --- | --- |
| Building raised | A new building is found. |
| Building upgraded | A building goes up a tier. |
| Spirit tier reached | The [Community Spirit](/guides/community-spirit/) of the village reaches a higher tier. |

Each deed goes to the player who reported the building with the MCA Blueprint. A change that no player reported is no player's deed. With MCA: Conversations installed, villagers gossip about these deeds.

### Chronicles

Reputation events become [Chronicles](/guides/chronicles/) entries: assaults, killings, cures, rescues, repelled raids, changes of reputation tier, granted titles, and resolved deeds.

## With Other Mods

- **[MCA: Crime](/compats/mca-crime/).** MCA: Crime also records its crimes in MCA: Reputation. When both mods are installed, Townstead takes deeds from MCA: Reputation only, so it records each deed once.
- **Vampirism.** During a village capture, and for 30 seconds after it, a player who hits or kills a villager in that capture gets no reputation deed for it. This needs MCA: Reputation 0.6.1 or later.
