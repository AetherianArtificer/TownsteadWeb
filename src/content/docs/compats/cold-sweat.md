## What It Adds

### Temperature

Cold Sweat turns on Townstead temperature. Villagers get a body temperature, dress for the weather, and warm up and cool down in rooms. See [Temperature](/guides/needs/temperature/) and [Temperature Mods](/climate/temperature-mods/).

- Clothing that Cold Sweat insulates, from any mod, keeps villagers warm or cool. Linings sewn into clothing count too.
- Cold Sweat food and drink that changes temperature also warms and cools villagers.
- Hearths, boilers, and iceboxes warm and cool rooms. See [Heat and Cooling Blocks](/climate/temperature-mods/#heat-and-cooling-blocks).
- Grace and Ice Resistance protect villagers, as for players.
- The temperature unit of Townstead and of Cold Sweat is one setting. A change to one changes the other.

### The Climatologist

With this mod, and without Tough As Nails, the Climatologist works in a Fuel Store or an Icehouse. Climatologists put fuel in hearths and boilers, and snow or ice in iceboxes. See [Tough As Nails](/compats/tough-as-nails/#the-climatologist).

## With Other Mods

- When more than one temperature mod is installed, Townstead uses Legendary Survival Overhaul, then Cold Sweat, then Tough As Nails. See [Tough As Nails](/compats/tough-as-nails/#with-other-mods).
- The Ecliptic Seasons: MultiMod Patch gives Cold Sweat the seasons of Ecliptic Seasons. See [Temperature Mods](/climate/temperature-mods/#ecliptic-seasons-compat-mods).
