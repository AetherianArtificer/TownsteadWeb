**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

### Buildings

| Building | Tiers | Who works there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tavern | Cocktail Counter, Tavern, Grand Tavern | Bartenders |
| Press House | One tier | Brewers |
| Tavern Cellar | One tier | Brewers |

Bartenders and Brewers are Paths of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/).

### Stations

| Station | Who uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Shaker, bar counter | Bartenders |
| Pressing tub, barrel | Brewers |

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a tavern hold Kaleidoscope Tavern drinks.

### Drinking

- Villagers drink the bottled drinks and cocktails of Kaleidoscope Tavern.
- Players who are tipsy from Kaleidoscope Tavern are also tipsy for Townstead.

### Hangouts

A tavern is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to ten villagers, for tastings and talk at the bar.
