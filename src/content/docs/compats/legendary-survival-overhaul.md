## What It Adds

### Temperature

Legendary Survival Overhaul turns on Townstead temperature. Villagers get a body temperature, dress for the weather, and warm up and cool down in rooms. See [Temperature](/guides/needs/temperature/) and [Temperature Mods](/climate/temperature-mods/).

- Legendary Survival Overhaul coats keep villagers warm or cool.
- Its warming and cooling food and drink also warm and cool villagers.
- A lit heater is a hearth for villagers. A lit cooler is a cool spot.
- Effects that the mod only gives to players also apply to villagers.

### Thirst

Legendary Survival Overhaul gives villagers thirst. See [Thirst](/guides/needs/thirst/).

- Villagers drink its canteens and drinks.
- Townstead drinks, such as the coffees and teas of supported mods, also fill the thirst bar of players.
- You can purify a dirty canteen on a campfire.

### The Climatologist

With this mod, and without Tough As Nails, the Climatologist works in a Fuel Store or an Icehouse. Climatologists keep the heaters and coolers of the village fuelled. See [Tough As Nails](/compats/tough-as-nails/#the-climatologist).

### Tailors

The sewing table makes the Tailor career available, and adds tailor's shops: Tailor's Corner, Tailor's Shop, and Clothier. Tailors sew at the sewing table, and sew coats onto armour. See [Tailor](/guides/tailor/).

## With Other Mods

- When more than one temperature mod or thirst mod is installed, Townstead uses Legendary Survival Overhaul first. See [Tough As Nails](/compats/tough-as-nails/#with-other-mods).
- With Ecliptic Seasons and the Serene Seasons API stub, Legendary Survival Overhaul counts each season twice. Townstead turns off its Serene Seasons copy. See [Temperature Mods](/climate/temperature-mods/#ecliptic-seasons-compat-mods).
