## What It Adds

### Temperature

Tough As Nails turns on Townstead temperature. Villagers get a body temperature, dress for the weather, and warm up and cool down in rooms. See [Temperature](/guides/needs/temperature/) and [Temperature Mods](/climate/temperature-mods/).

- Tough As Nails warming and cooling food and drink also warm and cool villagers.
- A thermoregulator that heats is a hearth for villagers. A thermoregulator that cools is a cool spot.
- Effects that Tough As Nails only gives to players also apply to villagers.

### Thirst

Tough As Nails gives villagers thirst. See [Thirst](/guides/needs/thirst/).

- Villagers drink Tough As Nails water bottles, canteens, and juices. Dirty water can make them sick.
- Villagers drink from a rain collector that has water in it.
- Townstead drinks, such as the coffees and teas of supported mods, also fill the thirst bar of players.
- You can boil a dirty water bottle on a campfire to purify it.

### The Climatologist

The Tough As Nails climatologist is a Townstead Climatologist. A villager becomes a Climatologist at a thermoregulator.

Climatologists keep the climate equipment of the village fuelled: thermoregulators, and the heaters, coolers, hearths, boilers, and iceboxes of the other temperature mods. A Climatologist can go on the **Frostkeeper**, **Heatwarden**, or **Stormcaller** Path.

### Baristas

Baristas make Tough As Nails juices at a crafting table. They purify water at a skillet, stove, or campfire, and in the water purifier.

## With Other Mods

- When more than one temperature mod is installed, Townstead uses Legendary Survival Overhaul, then Cold Sweat, then Tough As Nails. Set `preferredBackend` in `[needs.temperature]` to choose. See the [configuration](/reference/configuration/#temperature).
- When more than one thirst mod is installed, Townstead uses Legendary Survival Overhaul, then Thirst Was Reclaimed or Thirst Was Taken, then Tough As Nails. Set `preferredBackend` in `[needs.thirst]` to choose. See the [configuration](/reference/configuration/#thirst).
- If the thirst of Tough As Nails is turned off in its own settings, villagers have no thirst from it.
