## What It Adds

### Pizzerias

A pizzeria is a Cook workplace with three tiers:

1. Pizza Counter
2. Neighborhood Pizzeria
3. Ristorante Pizzeria

Cooks in a pizzeria go on the **Pizzaiolo** Path. The Path needs Farmer's Delight too.

### Making Pizza

| Station | What a Cook does |
| --- | --- |
| Basin | Makes cheese from milk and a fermenting item. |
| Pizza station | Puts the ingredients on a raw pizza. |
| Campfire or heat source | Bakes the raw pizza in place. |

A pizza with more different ingredients is better. Townstead gives each pizza a quality from its ingredients, as Pizza Delight does.

### Serving

A whole pizza on a [serving plate](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) is four slices. A pizza placed on a table also counts as served food. The plates in a pizzeria hold only pizza.
