**Install on:** The client of each player who wants it

## What It Adds

Traveler's Titles shows the name of a biome or dimension when you enter it. With Townstead, it also shows the village name when you walk into an MCA village:

- The title is the name of the village.
- The subtitle is the [Community Spirit](/guides/community-spirit/) of the village, such as *Harbor Town* or *Crossroads*. A village where no spirit has reached its first tier shows *Outpost*.

This title replaces the gold MCA welcome message above the hotbar. MCA's other actions when you enter a village still happen.

## Multiplayer

The server does not need Traveler's Titles. When you join, your client tells the server whether Traveler's Titles is installed. Players with it see the title. Players without it see the MCA welcome message.
