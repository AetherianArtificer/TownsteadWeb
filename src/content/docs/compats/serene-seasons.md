**Author:** TheAdubbz, Forstride\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/serene-seasons) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/serene-seasons)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **Calendar.** The calendar shows the Serene Seasons date. See [Calendars](/calendars/).
- **Climate.** The time of year, and the tropical wet and dry seasons, come from Serene Seasons. See [Seasons](/climate/seasons/).
- **Crops.** A crop in a Serene Seasons crop tag gets a climate class from that tag. Farmers follow its fertility rules, including greenhouse glass. See [Crops](/climate/crops/).
- **Snow.** Snow settles on Townstead blocks that are open to the sky, such as the serving plate, the calendar, and the Field Post, when it is cold and snowing. It melts in warm weather, in rain, and near bright light. When Ecliptic Seasons is also installed, Ecliptic Seasons draws this snow.

## With Other Mods

When TerraFirmaCraft or Ecliptic Seasons is also installed, the climate uses the first of TerraFirmaCraft, Ecliptic Seasons, and Serene Seasons. The calendar uses the first of TerraFirmaCraft, Serene Seasons, and Ecliptic Seasons.
