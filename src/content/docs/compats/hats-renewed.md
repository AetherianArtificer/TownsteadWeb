**Author:** metal_knight53\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/hats-renewed) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/hats-renewed)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **Hats on villagers.** A villager's Hats Renewed hat counts as an accessory that they wear.
- **Give Hat.** The **Give Hat** button on the villager's interaction menu gives the hat you are wearing to that villager. If the villager already wore a hat, you get it back.
- Hats are not on the Order Sheet. Tailors do not make them.
