## What It Adds

### The Government on the Charter

When a Capitals capital governs a village, the Charter of that village shows the Capitals government. A capital governs when it has a sovereign, its royal charter is not pending, and the village did not decline monarchy.

The Charter shows:

- the court offices, such as Sovereign, Heir, Hand of the Crown, and Lord Commander, and who holds them;
- your standing with the Crown, in hearts;
- the petitions that you can make, for lordship, a dukedom, Lord Commander, Hand of the Crown, or the throne.

To make a petition, you must have an audience with the sovereign near the Charter Bell. Capitals decides if it grants the title.

When a village has a pending royal charter, the Charter shows **Review Royal Charter**.

### Family Names

Townstead [cultures](/cultures/) and Capitals both give villagers family names. Townstead uses this order:

1. When the culture of a villager has a family-name rule, Townstead decides the family name. Townstead also writes it into Capitals, so the Capitals nameplate and Book of Houses show the same name.
2. When the culture has no family-name rule, the villager keeps the family name from Capitals.
3. A Decree of the House from Capitals changes the name, also when Townstead decided it.

Capitals draws the nameplate over villagers, so Townstead does not draw its own.
