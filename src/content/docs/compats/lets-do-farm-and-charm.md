**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

### The Cook Career

Farm & Charm makes the Cook career available. See [Cooking & Baking](/guides/cooking/).

### Stations

| Station | Who uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Cooking pot | Cooks and Bakers. Needs a heat source below it. |
| Roaster | Cooks. Needs a heat source below it. |
| Stove | Cooks and Bakers. |
| Crafting bowl | Cooks and Bakers. |
| Mincer | Cooks. |

### Farming

- Farmers plant Farm & Charm crops.
- Tomatoes climb a rope. The Farmer sets each tomato plant to climb. You hang the rope above it.
- Wild plants do not show in the [Field Post](/guides/farming/#the-field-post) crop list.

### Other

- Feeding troughs and pet bowls are not storage.
- Farm & Charm soups and teas give villagers water.
- When a temperature mod is installed, a lit stove or roaster is a heat source for the room.

## With Other Mods

- **[Let's Do: Bakery](/compats/lets-do-bakery/)** and **[Let's Do: Candlelight](/compats/lets-do-candlelight/)** need Farm & Charm.
