**Install on:** The client of each player who wants it

Just Enough Professions needs Just Enough Items.

## What It Adds

- **One entry per career.** A career that a building gives, such as the Cook, shows as one entry with its real workstations. Professions of other mods that join a Townstead career, such as the cook and chef of [Chef's Delight](/compats/chefs-delight/), do not show as separate jobs.
- **No false entries.** A special role that has no workstation does not show under every workstation.
- **MCA villagers.** The entries show an MCA villager in place of a vanilla villager.
