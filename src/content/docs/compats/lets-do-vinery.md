## What It Adds

### Buildings

| Building | Tiers | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Winery | Vintner's Shop, Winery, Vineyard Estate | The workplace of the **Winemaker** Path of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/). |
| Wine Cellar | One tier | Stores wine. |

A Vinery winemaker is a Townstead Winemaker. A villager can also become a Winemaker at a fermentation barrel, a grapevine pot, or an apple press.

### Drinking

Adult villagers drink Vinery wine and become tipsy.

### Hangouts

A Winery or a Vineyard Estate is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to six villagers, for rounds and talk.

### Farming

Farmers grow all eight kinds of grapes. They pick the grapes and do not break the bush.

## Settings

`teensMayDrinkAlcohol` in `[needs]` in the [server configuration](/reference/configuration/#needs) lets teens drink alcohol. Children never drink it.
