**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

### Buildings

| Building | Who works there |
| --- | --- |
| Taproom | Bartenders. An MCA inn is also a Bartender workplace. |
| Brewhouse, Working Brewhouse | Brewers. |

Bartenders and Brewers are Paths of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/).

### The Keg

Brewers and Bartenders brew drinks in the keg. They ferment the drink and then pour it into its container.

Townstead accepts both names of the drink mat: the item mat of older versions, and the coaster of newer versions.

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a taproom or an inn hold beer, mead, rice wine, and egg grog.

### Drinking

- Adult villagers drink beer and other Brewin' And Chewin' drinks, and become tipsy.
- Players who are tipsy from Brewin' And Chewin' are also tipsy for Townstead.

### Hangouts

A taproom is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to six villagers, for rounds, meals, and talk.

## With Other Mods

- **[Cocktails Delight](/compats/cocktails-delight/)** needs Brewin' And Chewin'.

## Settings

`teensMayDrinkAlcohol` in `[needs]` in the [server configuration](/reference/configuration/#needs) lets teens drink alcohol. Children never drink it.
