## What It Adds

### Buildings

| Building | Tiers | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beach cocktail bar | Cocktail Cabana, Beach Bar, Cocktail Lounge | The workplace of the **Sandy Merchant** Path of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/). |
| Beach Club | One tier | A hangout. Nobody works there. |

These buildings do not need walls.

A Beachparty sandy merchant or barkeeper is a Townstead Sandy Merchant.

### Stations

Sandy Merchants mix cocktails at the bar, and make ice cream in the mini fridge. Beachparty has a palm bar on 1.21.1, and a tiki bar on 1.20.1.

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a beach cocktail bar hold Beachparty cocktails.

### Drinking

Villagers drink Beachparty cocktails and eat its treats.

### Hangouts

- A beach cocktail bar is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to eight villagers, for rounds of cocktails.
- A Beach Club is a venue for up to eight villagers, to relax and listen to the radio.
- Villagers sit in Beachparty chairs, and lie on sun loungers, deck chairs, and beach towels.
