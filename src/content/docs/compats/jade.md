**Author:** Snownee\

**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

When you look at a villager or player, Jade shows:

- **Aspects.** One line for each aspect, such as *Vampire · Level 6*.
- **Offices.** The offices that they hold, such as *Presiding Councilor of Ashford*. Use this to find who speaks for a town.
- **Accord letters.** When you hold an accord letter and look at the person who speaks for a faction, Jade shows whether they would accept it, and why.
- **Growth.** Townstead removes Jade's growth timer from MCA villagers. Townstead sets their age from the calendar, so that timer would never count down.

## Pack Names

Jade shows which mod an item or block comes from. For a namespace from a pack, Jade shows a name that the pack gives in its language file, with the key `townstead.pack.<namespace>`:

```json
{
  "townstead.pack.examplepack": "Example Pack"
}
```
