## What It Adds

### Calendar and Climate

- The calendar shows the TerraFirmaCraft date. See [Calendars](/calendars/).
- In a world with the TerraFirmaCraft climate, Townstead uses its temperature. See [TerraFirmaCraft Worlds](/climate/seasons/#terrafirmacraft-worlds).

### Farming

- The TerraFirmaCraft composter is a Farmer job site, because TerraFirmaCraft has no vanilla composter recipe.
- Farmers grow TerraFirmaCraft crops: two-block crops, climbing crops on sticks, crops that they pick and that grow again, pumpkins and melons, and rice in flooded fields.
- Farmers fertilize with the TerraFirmaCraft fertilizers, by its own nutrient rules.
- Farmers clear dead crops and wild crops, and plant again with their seeds.
- Field Posts come in each TerraFirmaCraft wood. See [Farming](/guides/farming/#the-field-post).
