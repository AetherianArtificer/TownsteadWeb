title: "MCA: Crime"
description: "Crime events in Chronicles, and no charges during a village capture."
---

**Author:** Otectus\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mca-crime)\
**Install on:** The server

## What It Adds

### Chronicles

Crime events become [Chronicles](/guides/chronicles/) entries: reports, warrants, jail, release, kidnappings, freedom from captivity, and paid fines.

## With Other Mods

- **[MCA: Reputation](/compats/mca-reputation/).** When both mods are installed, Townstead takes deeds from MCA: Reputation, and takes only jail, custody, fines, warrants, and reports from MCA: Crime.
- **Vampirism.** During a village capture, and for 30 seconds after it, MCA: Crime does not charge a player who hits or kills a villager in that capture.
