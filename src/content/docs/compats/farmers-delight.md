## What It Adds

### The Cook Career

Farmer's Delight makes the Cook career available. Farm & Charm, Kaleidoscope Cookery, and Rustic Delight also do this. See [Cooking & Baking](/guides/cooking/).

### Kitchens

A kitchen is a Cook workplace with five tiers:

1. Hearth Kitchen
2. Prep Kitchen
3. Cookhouse
4. Chef's Kitchen
5. Grand Kitchen

Each higher tier seats more Cooks. The tiers use block groups, such as heat sources and storage, that each kitchen mod adds blocks to. So a kitchen works with any of the four kitchen mods. Open the [Catalog](/guides/catalog/) to see the blocks for each tier.

### Stations

| Station | What a Cook does |
| --- | --- |
| Cooking pot | Cooks meals. Needs a heat source below it. |
| Skillet | Cooks one stack at a time. Needs a heat source below it. |
| Stove | Cooks up to six items on its top. |
| Cutting board | Cuts ingredients with a knife from storage. |

Cooks do not take damage when they stand on a stove. They walk around cookware on a lit stove.

### Farming

- Farmers plant rice, tomatoes, onions, and cabbages.
- Knives count as harvest tools.
- Farmers clear sandy shrubs as weeds.
- Farmers put straw in storage.

### Other

- Cooks sell Farmer's Delight meals.
- When you take a meal from a cooking pot, serve it with a bowl, or finish food on a skillet, your Cook career gets the credit.
- Cooking pots, skillets, and stoves are not storage. Cutting boards are tool storage.
- When a temperature mod is installed, a lit stove is a heat source for the room.
