**Author:** DannyFather\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mca-descendants) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/mca-descendants)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

MCA Descendants lets a player continue as one of their descendants after death. Townstead does not change how MCA Descendants chooses or swaps the descendant. Townstead adds its own parts before and after the swap.

## What It Adds

- **A memorial of the old life.** When a player dies and continues as a descendant, Townstead records the old life with a memorial, as for a Townstead rebirth.
- **The Root and name of the descendant.** After the swap, the player has the Root, genes, heritage, and name of the descendant.
- **Names that agree.** The name that Townstead shows for the player follows the name in the MCA family tree.

## When Each Mod Handles a Death

MCA Descendants has a setting that limits it to hardcore worlds.

| World | What happens at death |
| --- | --- |
| Hardcore, or the MCA Descendants setting is off | MCA Descendants handles every death. Townstead rebirth is not available. |
| Not hardcore, and the MCA Descendants setting is on | The death screen shows **Continue as a descendant...**. Click it to continue as a descendant. Click **Respawn** to respawn as usual. |

If MCA Descendants cannot take over, the player respawns as usual and sees a message.
