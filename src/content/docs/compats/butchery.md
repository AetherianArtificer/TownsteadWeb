**Author:** jmods\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/butchery) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/butchery)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

Townstead needs Butchery 4.9 or later.

## What It Adds

Without Butchery, a Butcher only smokes meat. With Butchery, the Butcher does carcass work, grinding, taxidermy, curing, cleaning, and slaughter. See [Butchering](/guides/butchering/) for the full trade.

### Buildings

| Building | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Butcher's Stall, Butchery, Full Butchery | The three tiers of the Butcher workplace. |
| Slaughterhouse | Hooks and basins for carcass work. |
| Slaughter Pen | A fenced yard for the animals to slaughter. |
| Smokehouse | A room for smoking. |
| Tannery | A room for the Leatherworker. |

### Stations

| Station | What a Butcher does |
| --- | --- |
| Meat grinder | Grinds meat. |
| Pestle and mortar | Grinds ingredients. |
| Taxidermy table | Does taxidermy. |

### Other

- Butchery blocks are not storage, except the freezer.
- The Leatherworker tans hides. The Leatherworker tells you when they have no hide, salt, or wet sponge.
- Chronicles can record the first carcass that a Butcher works in a village.

## Settings

These settings are in the [server configuration](/reference/configuration/#profession-work).

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `allowLethalWork` | With `false`, Butchers do not slaughter. They do all other work. |
| `lethalWorkCooldownTicks` | The time between two slaughters. |
| `processTrophyOutputs` | With `true`, Butchers also break rare heads. |

## With Other Mods

- **[Farmer's Delight](/compats/farmers-delight/).** The meat grinder makes Farmer's Delight minced meat in place of Butchery minced meat.
