Townstead works with many other mods. MCA Reborn is required. All other mods on this page are optional. When a mod is not installed, Townstead turns off the parts that need it.

Each table gives what Townstead does with the mod, and where the mod must be installed for that to work:

- **Server**: on the server only.
- **Client**: on the client of each player who wants it.
- **Both**: on the server and on every client.

A mod can have its own requirements. Read the page of that mod too.

## MCA Reborn and Addons

MCA Reborn is required. The addons come from different authors.

| Mod | Author | What Townstead does | Install on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| [MCA Descendants](/compats/mca-descendants/) | DannyFather | Lets a player continue as a descendant. Townstead keeps a memorial of the old life and gives the player the Root and name of the descendant. | Both |
| [MCA Capitals](/compats/mca-capitals/) | MajesttyX | Shows the Capitals government on the Charter. Shares family names with Townstead cultures. | Both |
| [MCA: Reputation](/compats/mca-reputation/) | Otectus | Adds reputation to standing. Records Townstead deeds as reputation. Turns reputation events into Chronicles. | Server |
| [MCA: Crime](/compats/mca-crime/) | Otectus | Turns crime events into Chronicles. | Server |
| MCA: Quests | Otectus | Shows its quests in the Quest Ledger. MCA: Quests keeps its own tracking. | Both |
| MCA: Conversations | Otectus | Moves the text of dialogue choices to the right of its number badges. | Client |
