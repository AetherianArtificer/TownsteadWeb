## What It Adds

### The Cook Career

Rustic Delight makes the Cook career available. See [Cooking & Baking](/guides/cooking/).

### Cafés

With Farmer's Delight also installed, a café is a workplace for the Barista Path of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/). It has five tiers:

1. Corner Café
2. Coffee Shop
3. Coffee House
4. Café Hall
5. Grand Coffee House

Baristas brew drinks in a Farmer's Delight cooking pot. They roast coffee beans on a skillet, stove, or campfire.

### Coffee

Rustic Delight coffee gives villagers water and energy. Its syrup gives water.

### Farming

- Farmers plant cotton, bell peppers, and coffee beans.
- Farmers put cotton bolls in storage.
