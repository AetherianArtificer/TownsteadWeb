**Author:** TheIllusiveC4\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **Slots.** Villagers get the same Curios slots as players. A mod that adds a slot for players, such as a ring or backpack slot, adds it for villagers too.
- **Inventory.** The villager inventory screen shows the Curios slots in a panel at the side. Each slot has the Curios toggle to show or hide its item.
- **Clothing.** Villagers put clothing into Curios slots. Without Curios, clothing goes into a free armour slot. See [How Villagers Dress](/guides/tailor/#how-villagers-dress).
- **Necklace slot.** Townstead adds one necklace slot. The Townstead scarf goes there.
- **Drawing.** Curios items with their own renderer are drawn on villagers. Rigs can place Curios items too. See [Gear](/roots/rigs/gear/).

## With Other Mods

- **Backpacked.** Its backpack goes in the Curios back slot.
