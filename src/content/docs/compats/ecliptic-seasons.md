**Author:** joe_vettek, RoShioLeo, Sam_Chai\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ecliptic-seasons) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/ecliptic-seasons)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **Calendar.** The calendar shows the Ecliptic Seasons date. See [Calendars](/calendars/).
- **Climate.** The time of year comes from the 24 solar terms. Day length, monsoon rain, and where snow lies also come from Ecliptic Seasons. See [Seasons](/climate/seasons/).
- **Snow.** Ecliptic Seasons draws snow on Townstead blocks, such as the serving plate and the calendar.

## With Other Mods

- Ecliptic Seasons does not change the biome temperature that Cold Sweat and Tough As Nails read. Two other mods give them its seasons. See [Temperature Mods](/climate/temperature-mods/#ecliptic-seasons-compat-mods).
- With the Serene Seasons API stub, other mods see Ecliptic Seasons as Serene Seasons. Townstead still reads Ecliptic Seasons directly.
