## What It Adds

### Stew Pots

Cooks make soup in the stew pot and the lead stew pot:

1. The Cook puts water and ingredients in the pot.
2. The pot cooks the soup.
3. The Cook fills bowls from the pot, and takes out each bowl and empty bucket.

Each bowl of soup keeps the ingredients that went into it, as when a player fills it.

### Other

- When a temperature mod is installed, a lit kitchen stove is a heat source for the room.
- Snails and the fruits of trees and bushes do not show in the [Field Post](/guides/farming/#the-field-post) crop list. They do not grow on farmland.
