title: Whole Cloth
description: "Cloth scraps from villagers' kills, and scrap blocks as fabric."
---

**Author:** davigj\
**Download:** [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/whole-cloth)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **Cloth scraps.** Whole Cloth gives cloth scraps only when a player kills a mob. When a villager, such as a guard, kills a mob that drops cloth, the villager can also get a scrap, at the same chance. The villager puts it in storage.
- **Fabric.** Whole Cloth scrap blocks count as fabric for a tailor's shop. See [Tailor](/guides/tailor/).

## With Other Mods

- **[Weaver's Paradise](/compats/weavers-paradise/).** Four cloth scraps and an empty spool make a cotton spool at the spinning jenny.
