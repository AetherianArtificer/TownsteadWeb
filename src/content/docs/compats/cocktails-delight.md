**Author:** DraconicArcher\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cocktails-delight) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/cocktails-delight)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

Cocktails Delight needs [Brewin' And Chewin'](/compats/brewin-and-chewin/). Its mod ID is `brewincompatdelight` on 1.20.1 and `cocktailsdelight` on 1.21.1. Townstead supports both.

## What It Adds

- Bartenders mix its cocktails at a crafting table.
- The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a taproom or an inn also hold vodka tonics and boilermakers.
- Adult villagers drink its cocktails and become tipsy. Villagers of all ages can drink its soft drinks.
