**Author:** thedoctorofdoctoring\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/vampiric-ageing-a-vampirism-addon)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

Vampiric Ageing needs [Vampirism](/compats/vampirism/).

## What It Adds

Vampiric Ageing gives older vampires an age rank from 1 to 5. Townstead reads the rank of a vampire player and sets their Townstead vampire level above the levels of Vampirism:

| Age rank | Townstead vampire level |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 15 |
| 2 | 16 |
| 3 | 17 |
| 4 | 18 |
| 5 | 19 |
