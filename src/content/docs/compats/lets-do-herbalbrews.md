## What It Adds

### Buildings

| Building | Tiers | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tea house | Tea Stall, Tea Room, Tea House | A workplace of the **Barista** Path of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/). |
| Herbal Dryhouse | One tier | A Barista workplace. |
| Witch hut | Witch's Hut, Witch's Cottage | The workplace of the Witch. |
| Herb garden | Herb Plot, Herbal Garden | A garden. Nobody works there. |

### Stations

- Baristas brew tea and coffee in the tea kettle and the copper tea kettle.
- Witches work at the HerbalBrews cauldron, and can wear the witch hat.

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a tea house hold HerbalBrews teas and coffee.

### Drinking

Tea gives villagers water. Coffee gives them water and energy.

### Hangouts

A tea house is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to six villagers, for tea, coffee breaks, tastings, and talk. Villagers sit on carpets as tea mats.
