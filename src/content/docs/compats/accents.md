**Author:** Obscuria\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/accents) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/accents)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **The Tailor career.** Accents makes the Tailor career available, and adds tailor's shops: Tailor's Corner, Tailor's Shop, and Clothier. See [Tailor](/guides/tailor/).
- **The sewing station.** Tailors sew hoods, scarves, hats, and accessories at it. You can order each one on the [Order Sheet](/guides/orders/).
- **Clothing.** Villagers wear Accents pieces. The warm pieces and the sun hats change temperature, so villagers wear them in cold or hot weather.
