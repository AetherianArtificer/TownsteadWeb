**Install on:** The server and every client

Let's Do: Bakery needs [Farm & Charm](/compats/lets-do-farm-and-charm/).

## What It Adds

### Bakeries

Without this mod, the Bakery is a building with a crafting table and an oven. With it, the Baker gets three more workplaces:

1. Bread Stand
2. Bake Sale
3. Bakery

See [Baking](/guides/cooking/#baking).

### Stations

| Station | What a Baker does |
| --- | --- |
| Baker station | Makes baked goods. |
| Small cooking pot | Cooks. Needs a heat source below it. |

### Paths

With this mod, a Baker can go on the **Jam Maker** or **Pastry Chef** Path.

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a bakery hold only baked goods, from Minecraft and from this mod.

### Kitchen Sinks

Villagers drink from a filled kitchen sink. It gives them water.
