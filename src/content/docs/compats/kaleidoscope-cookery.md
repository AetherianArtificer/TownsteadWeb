**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

### The Cook Career

Kaleidoscope Cookery makes the Cook career available. See [Cooking & Baking](/guides/cooking/).

### Stations

| Station | What a Cook does |
| --- | --- |
| Pot | Cooks meals. Needs a heat source below it. |
| Stockpot | Cooks soup with water. Needs a heat source below it. |
| Steamer | Steams food. Needs a heat source below the stack of steamers. |
| Millstone | Grinds ingredients. A villager or work animal must turn it. |
| Chopping board | Cuts ingredients with a kitchen knife from storage. |

### The Mill

The Mill is a building with a millstone. Cooks and Bakers work in it.

With this mod, a Baker can go on the **Miller** Path. Millers work at the millstone.

Villagers and work animals, such as horses and donkeys, can turn a millstone.

### Other

When a temperature mod is installed, a lit Kaleidoscope Cookery stove is a heat source for the room.
