## What It Adds

### Buildings

| Building | Tiers | Who works there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brew hall | Taproom, Alehouse, Beer Hall | Bartenders |
| Brewhouse | Mash House, Copper House, Brewery Works | Brewers |
| Cask Cellar | One tier | Nobody. It stores brewed drinks, and keeps them cool when a temperature mod is installed. |

Bartenders and Brewers are Paths of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/).

### Brewing Stations

Brewers and Bartenders brew at the wooden, copper, and netherite brewing stations. A better station brews more kinds of drink.

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a brew hall hold Brewery beers.

### Drinking

- Adult villagers drink Brewery beer and become tipsy.
- Players who are drunk from Brewery are also tipsy, drunk, or wasted for Townstead, by how drunk they are.

### Hangouts

A brew hall is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to ten villagers, for rounds, meals, and talk. Villagers sit on benches.

### Farming

Farmers grow hops. Hops climb a rope. The Farmer sets each plant to climb. You hang the rope above it.

## Settings

`teensMayDrinkAlcohol` in `[needs]` in the [server configuration](/reference/configuration/#needs) lets teens drink alcohol. Children never drink it.
