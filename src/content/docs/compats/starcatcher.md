**Author:** wdiscute\
**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/starcatcher) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/starcatcher)\
**Install on:** The server and every client

## What It Adds

- **Rods.** Starcatcher rods count as fishing rods for a Fisherman. See [Fishing](/guides/fishing/).
- **Fish.** A Fisherman who fishes with a Starcatcher rod catches Starcatcher fish, by the chances of Starcatcher's own fish data. Starcatcher's minigame is only for players.
- **Skills.** Fisherman Skills that work with a fishing rod, such as **Barbed Hook** and **Tangle**, also work with a Starcatcher rod.

Townstead supports Starcatcher 2 and Starcatcher 3.
