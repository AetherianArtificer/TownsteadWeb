## What It Adds

### Restaurants

A restaurant has three tiers:

1. Eatery
2. Bistro
3. Restaurant

A restaurant is a workplace for Cooks and for the Bartender Path of the [Beverage Artisan](/guides/beverage-artisan/). Each higher tier seats more of both.

### Stations

Cooks use the Candlelight cooking pot, cooking pan, and stoves. The pot and the pan need a heat source below them.

### Serving

The [serving plates](/guides/cooking/#serving-food) in a restaurant hold only Candlelight dishes.

### Hangouts

A restaurant is a [hangout](/guides/hangouts/) venue for up to eight villagers. They go there for dinner and for time together after dinner.

### Other

When a temperature mod is installed, a lit Candlelight stove is a heat source for the room.
